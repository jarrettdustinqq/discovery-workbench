What this does not establish

    JSON includes the dataset, SHA-256, split membership, full-precision coefficients, candidate scores, proposed measurement, and limitations. It is a record—not an independently signed attestation.

    A discovery instrument, not a claim of superintelligence.

    It searches a fixed grammar of candidate representations, combines useful terms, retains competing explanations, and asks where they disagree. No frontier model runs behind this page. Synthetic examples demonstrate software behavior; they are not new scientific discoveries. Repeated tuning after seeing test results requires genuinely new validation data.
